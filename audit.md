# dductly Product Audit

## AI Receipt & Invoice Capture

### 1. What I looked at

I spent some time going through dductly from a user's perspective and looking for areas where the experience could be made easier or more seamless.

One thing that stood out to me was the process of entering information from receipts and invoices.

The current process works, but I think there is an opportunity to take some of the manual work out of it.

---

## 2. The Problem

When a business has a receipt or invoice, they currently need to look through the document and manually enter the relevant information into dductly.

That means the user has to:

1. Look through the document
2. Find the information they need
3. Enter it into the right fields
4. Double-check everything
5. Save the transaction
6. Keep track of the original document

Doing this once isn't a huge deal, but when a business is doing it over and over, it can become a pretty repetitive process.

### Why I think this is worth looking into

- It takes time
- There is room for manual entry mistakes
- Receipts and invoices can all look different
- Users still need to keep the original document

---

## 3. What I'd Want to Know

Before jumping straight into a solution, I'd want to understand how big of a problem this actually is.

Some of the things I'd look at:

- How many receipts and invoices are being entered?
- How long does the process usually take?
- How often do users go back and correct information?
- Which fields are most commonly corrected?
- Where do users tend to drop off?
- What are users saying about the current process?

This would help me understand whether this is a problem worth prioritizing and where the biggest opportunities are.

---

## 4. What I Think the Bigger Problem Is

The issue isn't necessarily that the form itself is difficult to use.

The bigger issue is that the user is taking information from an unstructured document and manually turning it into structured data.

That's a lot of work that could potentially be handled for them.

---

## 5. My Idea

### AI Receipt & Invoice Capture

Instead of manually entering everything, a user could simply upload a receipt or invoice and let AI do the initial work.

The experience would look something like:

```text
Upload / Take Photo
        ↓
      AI reads it
        ↓
Information is extracted
        ↓
    User reviews
        ↓
 Make any corrections
        ↓
   Save transaction
```
